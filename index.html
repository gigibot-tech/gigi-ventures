01 — The words

We change the words. Then we change the products.

Call people “users” and their evenings “engagement”, and you’ll end up building a slot machine. So we started with a dictionary.

Three rules. For every leaf.

  1. 01

    People

06 — Health · matching Live · Germany pilot

GiGi × YourHealthMatch. A doctor who speaks your language.

Language—insurance—place—then a time.

International students, expats and newly arrived workers in Germany tell us the language they need, their insurance and where they are. We show the practices that fit — and clinics open their own times. The match is the product; a video visit is only one way to hold an appointment.

Join the live waitlist ↗ Try the match questionnaire ↗

Both open yourhealthmatch.de in a new tab. The waitlist takes an email and whether you are a patient or a clinic; the questionnaire is a demo profile — language, insurance, place, visit type, time — and what you enter stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to GiGi Ventures.

GiGi × YHM

Partner · live

YourHealthMatch is deployed at yourhealthmatch.de — a static SvelteKit app with Netlify functions. Not a medical practice, not emergency care.

AI-ready, not AI-dependent. Today the match runs on language, insurance and place — rules you can read. GiGi AI could add language help and ranking later: an idea, not a feature.

yourhealthmatch.de ↗

  1. Find a doctorLanguage, insurance and place in; practices that fit out.
  2. Book a timeTake a time the clinic has opened — or open Doctolib, Doctena and similar, which keep their own appointments.
  3. Set up the clinicA practice enters its profile, schedule and open times, so patients can book them.
  4. Hold the visitIf the appointment is a video visit, the doctor opens the room. If video fails, the booking stays.
