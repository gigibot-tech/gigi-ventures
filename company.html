About

Why GiGi Ventures exists

The most-used software in the world is designed to be hard to put down. We think Europe can build the alternative.

GiGi Ventures builds products that earn their place in people’s lives: useful on their own, honest about how they work, and connected into one ecosystem so that each new product makes the others better. We grow bottom-up — one small, proven product at a time — instead of building a platform first and hoping people come.

“We measure success in hours given back, not hours captured.”

  • 1

    People

    Built for the person, not the session. We count what happens offline: words learned, meetups that happened, meals served.

  • 2

    Explainable

    Every ranking and every AI answer can tell you why. Our feeds end, and we try to detect dopamine dips instead of exploiting them.

  • 3

    Private & interoperable by default

    Data stays on your device where it can — GiGi AI runs there too — and moves with you, to our apps or anyone else’s.

How we work

From one leaf to an ecosystem

  1. 01

    Find a real problem

    We start where people struggle offline — learning, meeting, building, eating — not where attention is cheap.

  2. 02

    Ship a small, useful leaf

    One product that works on its own. Simple, fast, honest about how it works.

  3. 03

    Measure what happens offline

    Words learned. Meetups that happened. Meals served. Not time-in-app.

  4. 04

    Connect and scale

    Proven leaves join GiGi Social. Automation and AI arrive only where a bottleneck is measured.

Our decision rule

Technology follows evidence.

In our food concept that means a €500 grain cooker before a €250,000 robot. In our apps it means plain rules before a model, and a model on your device before one in our cloud.

Careers

Join GiGi Ventures

We’re a small team building something big — carefully. We hire for judgement and kindness, and we care what happens after people close our apps.

Who thrives here

  • You’d rather delete a feature than add a dark pattern.
  • You explain complex things simply — to users and teammates.
  • You’ve built something end to end: an app, a community, a kitchen.
  • You care about what happens offline.
  • You’re comfortable with early-stage ambiguity.

What you’ll get*

  • Ownership from your first week: products, not tickets.
  • Remote-friendly across Europe, with time together in Berlin.
  • Work you can explain to your family — and use yourself.
  • A studio where every product shares the same three rules.
